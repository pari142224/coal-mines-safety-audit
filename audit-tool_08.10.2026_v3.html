Of standard is the score card figure — points earned out of the full 2420 the standard carries. It is the formal result, and it reads low until the audit is finished. Of assessed is points earned out of what has actually been assessed, which is the figure to work to during the audit. Every check point carries 5 points; N/A earns all 5.

AI assist — Gemini

Polishes the wording of your observations and drafts corrective actions. It rewrites what you have written — it never sees the plant, so every suggestion must be read and approved by you before it goes into the report.

Get one free at aistudio.google.com/apikey. The key is held on this device only and is never written into saved audits or exported reports. Restrict it to the Generative Language API in Google Cloud, and don't share this HTML file after entering a key on a shared machine.

Gemini may cite only what you list here, and may use a clause or rule number only if you write that number into the line yourself. Anything else is referred to by title alone. This is deliberate — models fabricate clause numbers readily, and a wrong citation in a report that reaches a regulator is a serious problem. Add your own site-specific consents and standards to this list.

Gemini 3 Flash is still a preview model — Google deprecates those with two weeks' notice, so a stable model is the safer default for a tool you'll use over a long period.
